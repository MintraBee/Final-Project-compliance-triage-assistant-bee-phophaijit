# Compliance Tracker Assistant

## 1. Project Context

In regulated industries such as biotechnology, healthcare, and life sciences, compliance issues are often surfaced through internal meeting notes, audit findings, HR discussions, legal reviews, or operational conversations.

These updates are usually written in unstructured language and may involve multiple stakeholders.

Examples include:
- payroll registration issues for remote employees
- biosafety inspection delays
- privacy or HIPAA-related partner inquiries
- contract approval bottlenecks
- overdue policy reviews

When these issues are not properly tracked, organizations may face operational delays, audit findings, financial penalties, or regulatory risk.

---

## 2. Target User

The primary user is:

A compliance coordinator, project coordinator, operations manager, or cross-functional program manager working in a regulated organization.

This user often receives incomplete or messy compliance notes and must turn them into actionable follow-up items.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Today, coordinators often complete this process manually:

1. Read meeting notes
2. Identify the compliance domain
3. Determine the responsible owner
4. Search for relevant internal policy or SOP
5. Decide escalation priority
6. Create a tracker-ready action item

This process is:
- time consuming
- inconsistent
- dependent on tribal knowledge
- vulnerable to missed ownership or escalation

---

## 4. Proposed Workflow

This assistant supports first-pass compliance triage.

### Input:
One unstructured compliance note

### Output:
A structured triage report including:

- Domain
- Issue Summary
- Suggested Owner
- Escalation Level
- Policy Reference
- Recommended Next Action
- Human Review Requirement

---

## 5. Human Oversight

This system does not provide legal advice.

This system does not make final compliance decisions.

All outputs require human review before any action is taken.

---

## 6. Business Value

The assistant helps organizations:

- reduce manual triage time
- improve consistency across teams
- reduce missed ownership gaps
- improve audit readiness
- create tracker-ready outputs faster
